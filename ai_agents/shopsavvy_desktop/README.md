# ShopSavvy Desktop

An autonomous AI shopping agent for Mac, Windows, and Linux that auto-buys products at target prices, navigates retailer checkouts, and monitors prices in the background.

## Features

- **Auto-Buy** — Set a target price and the agent automatically purchases when the price drops
- **Checkout Agent** — Navigates retailer websites and completes checkout autonomously
- **Background Price Monitoring** — Tracks prices across thousands of retailers even when the app is closed
- **Menu Bar Integration** — Quick access from the system tray/menu bar
- **Call for Me** — Voice agent that calls stores to check stock and negotiate price matching
- **Real-Time Price Comparison** — Compare prices across 70,000+ retailers for any product

## Platforms

- macOS (Mac App Store and direct download)
- Windows
- Linux

## About ShopSavvy

ShopSavvy is a free price comparison app that has been around since 2008 with over 40 million downloads. The desktop app brings full AI agent capabilities to the shopping experience — it doesn't just show you prices, it acts on your behalf to get you the best deal.
